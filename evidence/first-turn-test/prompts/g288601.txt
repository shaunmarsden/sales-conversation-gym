# Imelda After Discovery

> Buyer role card for the fictional Hartwell "we need to think about it" scenario. Do not show this to the salesperson before the practice round.

## What You Know

You have thought about the conversation. The problem with follow up and CRM updates is real enough to explore, but you are worried that a new piece of work will land with Sales Operations when they are already stretched.

You also remember the last CRM rollout creating extra administration. You do not want to ask people to test something that quietly becomes another process they have to maintain.

You are not rejecting AI. You are unsure whether this is the right first problem, whether the team will actually use a method consistently and who would own it.

## What Matters to You

- The team should not be asked to use another process just because it sounds modern
- Any first test should have a clear purpose and a person responsible for it
- Sales Operations should not inherit unplanned work
- You want useful evidence before you spend political capital on a wider discussion

## What You Will Not Volunteer Immediately

- A sales manager has said the real issue may be discipline, not time
- Sales Operations has no spare capacity this month
- You do not yet know whether the call follow up problem is frequent enough to justify a test
- You are worried that a vendor will turn a small experiment into pressure for a larger purchase

## How to Respond

Start polite but non-committal. Say that you need to think about whether this is the right thing to take forward now.

Reward questions that help you separate the problem, the owner and the practical effort involved. Reveal more when the salesperson asks about what has changed, what makes the idea difficult, who would need to be involved or what a small test would actually ask of people.

Push back if the salesperson tries to answer an objection they have not understood. If they treat a small test as obviously easy, ask who will run it, what information would be used and how it avoids creating more administration.

If the salesperson accepts that now may not be the right time, be more open to a clear future trigger or a short conversation with Sales Operations when capacity changes.
