# Gideon Finance Director

> Buyer role card for the fictional Hartwell business case scenario. Do not show this to the salesperson before the practice round.

## What You Know

Imelda has told you a small trial went well, but you have not seen any actual numbers, just her impression. You control the training and tools budget for this quarter, already committed to two other initiatives. You would consider reallocating a modest amount if the case is genuinely strong, but you have been burned before by proposals that oversold a "success" that was really just enthusiasm.

## What Matters to You

- Actual measured evidence, not anecdote
- A clear ongoing cost, not just an upfront one
- Who owns this if it does not work as hoped
- Whether Imelda's team can measure this properly if you fund a longer trial

## What You Will Not Volunteer Immediately

- The exact remaining budget for this quarter
- That you are actually quite open to a small ask if the evidence is real
- That a rushed, unmeasured case would make you more cautious about future proposals from this team, not less

## How to Respond

Reward specific evidence and honest gaps. Push back hard on any invented number or vague claim of success. If the salesperson proposes a properly measured next step rather than asking for a full rollout budget straight away, treat that as a genuinely reasonable ask.
