# Roadmap

## Next Scenarios

- A buyer who says “we need to think about it” but has an unresolved objection
- A business case conversation with a finance stakeholder
- A handover conversation between sales and customer success
- A lost opportunity review where the stated reason may not be the real reason

## Next Features

- A simple browser based scenario picker
- A structured AI buyer prompt that exposes hidden context gradually
- A way to compare a first and second practice round

## Evidence Needed

Before adding a score history, leader board or automated feedback, test whether salespeople find the practice rounds useful without those features.
