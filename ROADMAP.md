# Roadmap

## Next Scenarios

- A handover conversation between sales and customer success
- A lost opportunity review where the stated reason may not be the real reason

## Next Features

- A simple browser based scenario picker
- A way to compare a first and second practice round

## Evidence Needed

Before adding a score history, leader board or automated feedback, test whether salespeople find the practice rounds useful without those features. The new AI buyer prompt also needs testing against a real round before it is trusted: an AI can misjudge when a question has genuinely earned a piece of hidden context.

## Built So Far

- A discovery conversation where the buyer's context is still unclear
- A follow up conversation where "we need to think about it" hides a practical concern about ownership, capacity and effort
- A business case conversation where a commercial sponsor's enthusiasm is not the same as finance approval, and no trial result has actually been measured
- A structured AI buyer prompt that exposes hidden context gradually, for practising without a second person
