## Evidence Needed

Before adding a score history, leader board or automated feedback, test whether salespeople find the practice rounds useful without those features.

The AI buyer prompt was run against one real live round on 5 August 2026, Hartwell First Discovery with the Meera Commercial Director role card, not a fictional description of how it should behave. Gradual reveal held: a vague question got a vague answer, a specific question earned the hidden detail it was actually aimed at. An unsupported cost claim was challenged and tied back to specific detail already on the table, not a generic objection. The round ended and gave feedback on cue, including naming a real gap in the proposed test and surfacing, honestly and only in the debrief, two things the salesperson never actually earned during the round. One open question: a completely fresh first turn showed a brief break in character before settling in for the rest of the round; every turn after that, once framed as a continuing conversation, held character cleanly. This is one test run by the person who built it, not an independent salesperson, so it raises confidence in the mechanism without being independent evidence that it works for someone else.
