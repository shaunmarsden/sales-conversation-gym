# Hartwell Business Case Conversation

> This scenario is fictional. Hartwell and every person in it are invented.

## What the Salesperson Knows

Following the earlier discovery conversation, Meera Kapoor, Commercial Director, agreed to a small internal trial: three sales managers used an AI tool to draft follow up notes and speed up CRM updates for two weeks. Meera has asked you to help her put together a business case for Daniel Osei, the Finance Director, so the wider team can get budget approval.

You do not know Hartwell's actual budget cycle, what Daniel's approval threshold is, or whether the trial's results have been formally measured yet.

## Your Job

Help build a business case Meera can actually take to Daniel, using only what the trial has genuinely shown so far. Do not invent a saving figure, a payback period or a budget Daniel has not confirmed.

## What You Should Not Assume

- The trial produced a measured time saving
- Daniel has a fixed budget already set aside for this
- Daniel's approval is a formality once Meera is on board
- A wider rollout has been agreed in principle

## Good Outcome

By the end, you know what the trial can and cannot actually claim, what Daniel would need to see to make a decision, and whether a further, better measured trial is needed before the case is strong enough to take to him.
